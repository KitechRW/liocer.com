# LIOCER Website — Content and Structure

18 November 2026

## Website purpose

Introduce LIOCER as a software engineering company focused on SaaS products and open to custom software projects. Help visitors understand the offering and take one of three actions: explore a product, discuss a project, or propose a partnership.

Brand slogan: **Digital solutions that move business forward.**

| Navigation | Route | Purpose | Main action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home | / | Introduce LIOCER and its two routes to working together | Explore products / Discuss a project |

## Visual and interaction direction

### Brand expression

- Use the existing LIOCER ribbon logo and navy identity.
- Combine LIOCER Navy (#092B5B), white (#FFFFFF), Ice (#F3F6FA) and Slate (#52647A) with a restrained tonal blue accent (#7396C4).
- Use Manrope 600/700 for headlines and Inter 400/500/600 for body and interface text, with generous spacing and a consistent content width.
- Echo the ribbon’s shape in subtle backgrounds and transitions.
- Use real product previews, labeled interface concepts and purposeful abstract visuals.

### Layout

- Alternate open editorial sections with a few substantial product and service panels.
- Give KLEX a distinctive feature area while preserving LIOCER’s broader software engineering identity.
- Keep product and custom-project paths easy to reach from the homepage.
- Use compact supporting copy with a clear next action in each major section.

### Interaction

- Use subtle hover states and restrained scroll reveals.
- Maintain visible keyboard focus, readable contrast and reduced-motion support.
- Make the mobile navigation and enquiry form easy to operate by touch.
- Keep core content visible when animation is disabled.
- Preselect the appropriate enquiry category when a visitor follows a product or project CTA.
